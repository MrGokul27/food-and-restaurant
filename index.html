How We Craft It

From British Pastures To Your Plate

01
Dawn Farm Harvest

Our produce arrives at 6:00 AM daily from heritage growers across Kent and Sussex.

02
48-Hr Fermentation

Our Neapolitan pizza dough and brioche buns rest for 48 hours for airy digestibility.

03
450°C Oak-Fired Searing

Pizzas bake in 90 seconds over kiln-dried British oak while patties sear on cast iron.

04
Served Piping Hot

Plated immediately for our dining room or dispatched in thermal bags within minutes.
